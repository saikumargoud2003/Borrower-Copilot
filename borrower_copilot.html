Borrower Copilot

System Architecture, Financial Domain Logic & Interview Defense Guide

Lokta Build Challenge

1. Problem Gap & Core Architecture

Lenders maintain institutional risk models (FOIR caps, risk-based rate matrices) designed exclusively to optimize bank profitability. The Indian retail borrower arrives without an objective reference point, frequently overpaying by 300–400 bps or committing to unsustainable EMIs[cite: 1]. Borrower Copilot reverses this dynamic by acting as an uncompromised borrower-side advisory engine[cite: 1].

Architecture Paradigm: A decoupled MERN pattern with zero-persistence, client-side resilience, and deterministic rules execution[cite: 1].

  • Client (React + Vite + Tailwind): Mobile-first negotiation viewport designed to function as an in-branch negotiation asset on a smartphone[cite: 1].
  • Credit Engine (TypeScript / Express): Pure functional domain modules with zero external bureau dependencies[cite: 1]. Runs calculations deterministically in milliseconds[cite: 1].
  • Local Fallback Architecture: Defaults to in-memory JSON fixtures, guaranteeing evaluation runs from README in under 5 minutes without mandatory MongoDB service setup[cite: 1].

2. The 4 Evaluated Outputs (Design & Rules)

Output 01: The Verdict Engine

Determines whether borrowing is structurally sound[cite: 1]. "Don't Borrow" is a mandatory circuit breaker[cite: 1]:

  • Don't Borrow: Fires if borrower has ≥1 bounced EMI in the last 12 months combined with active high-interest app loans (>30%), or if existing FOIR already exceeds 60% of income[cite: 1]. Prevents predatory debt compounding[cite: 1].
  • Borrow Less: Fires when a requested loan is for depreciating/lifestyle consumption (e.g., weddings) and the requested amount severely drains monthly emergency buffers[cite: 1].
  • Borrow: Fires when the loan is productive (business expansion or income asset) and total obligations remain ≤40% FOIR[cite: 1].

Output 02: Dual Capacity Engine (Sanction vs. Safe)

Distinguishes what institutional banks will approve from what a household budget can safely sustain[cite: 1]:

  • Lender Sanction: Computed via traditional banking FOIR:
    Allowed EMI = (Net Monthly Income × Standard FOIR) - Existing EMIs
    Salaried FOIR is capped at 50%[cite: 1]. Self-employed unsecured FOIR is capped strictly at 40% of verifiable ITR income (ignoring cash)[cite: 1].
  • Safe Capacity: Computed via residual household cashflow after a 40% discretionary safety reserve:
    Safe Surplus = Net Income + (Spouse Income × 0.7) - Living Costs - Existing EMIs
    Safe EMI Ceiling = Safe Surplus × 0.60
    Safe capacity is the Present Value (PV) of this safe EMI, ensuring the borrower is protected against unexpected shocks[cite: 1].

Output 03: Fair Interest Rate & RBI All-In APR Band

Expressed as an honest risk-spread band rather than an arbitrary single point[cite: 1]:

  • Base Anchor: 6.50% RBI Repo floor. Risk premiums are systematically stacked based on employment tier, collateral backing, and CIBIL score[cite: 1].
  • Unknown Score Rule: If credit score is unknown, the model widens the rate spread by ±1.75% rather than defaulting to subprime 300, explicitly warning the borrower of unverified pricing uncertainty[cite: 1].
  • All-In APR Transparency: Mandates inclusion of upfront processing fees (1.5%–2.5%) plus 18% GST over loan tenure, exposing hidden origination costs[cite: 1].

Output 04: Max Safe EMI & Stress Testing

Provides a non-negotiable monthly outflow ceiling alongside two real-world stress simulations[cite: 1]:

  • Rate Shock (+200 bps): Models the cashflow impact if floating benchmark rates jump by +2.0%[cite: 1].
  • Income Shock (-20%): Assesses whether the household budget plunges into negative cashflow during a 20% earnings contraction[cite: 1].

4. Defense of the Three Test Archetypes

Priya (29, Bengaluru) — MNC Software Engineer Verdict: Borrow Less

Profile: Net ₹1,10,000/mo. Rent: ₹28,000. Car EMI: ₹14,000. CIBIL: 780. Wants ₹18L personal loan for wedding[cite: 1].

  • Sanction vs Safe Divergence: Lenders sanction ~₹16.5L–18L based purely on 50% FOIR limits[cite: 1]. However, taking ₹18L adds an EMI of ~₹39,000, which reduces her monthly savings buffer to nearly zero[cite: 1].
  • Copilot Output: Recommends a safe capacity ceiling of ₹10L–12L[cite: 1].
  • Negotiation Card Defense: Prime CIBIL (780) and MNC tier entitle her to 10.5%–11.5%[cite: 1]. If a branch quotes 14%, she counters with her prime bureau standing and limits borrowing to safe reserves[cite: 1].
Ravi (42, Mysuru) — Kirana Store Owner Verdict: Borrow (Route to LAP)

Profile: Cash: ₹40k–80k/mo. ITR: ₹4.2L/yr (₹35k/mo). No credit history. Owns shop worth ₹45L. Wants ₹15L for expansion[cite: 1].

  • Product Rerouting: Applying for an unsecured business loan results in rejection or a tiny sanction (~₹5.2L) at 16%+ because banks only evaluate his ₹4.2L ITR[cite: 1].
  • Copilot Output: Pivots Ravi to a Loan Against Property (LAP) backed by his ₹45L shop, unlocking up to ₹22.5L capacity at 9.5%–10.5% over a 10-year repayment structure[cite: 1].
  • Negotiation Card Defense: Advises Ravi to refuse unsecured loans and propose LAP directly using his commercial premises as collateral[cite: 1].
Anita (35, Hubballi) — Gig Delivery & Tailor Verdict: Don't Borrow

Profile: Net: ₹26k–30k/mo. Husband unemployed. 3 app loans totaling ₹35,000 at 30%+, 1 bounced EMI. Wants ₹1.5L for EV[cite: 1].

  • Circuit Breaker Trigger: Anita is trapped in high-interest rollover debt[cite: 1]. Adding ₹1.5L of debt with an unstable single-earner income creates severe risk of total insolvency[cite: 1].
  • Copilot Output: Declares an unequivocal "Don't Borrow"[cite: 1]. Safe new borrowing capacity is ₹0[cite: 1].
  • Negotiation Card Defense: Recommends prioritizing the closure of 30%+ app loans before taking fresh debt, or exploring zero-down EV fleet leasing programs instead of personal micro-loans[cite: 1].

5. Live Interview Defense & Live Modification Strategy

During the live technical evaluation, assessors will ask you to justify trade-offs and modify credit assumptions live[cite: 1]:

  • Explainability Anchor: Every single number rendered on the UI traces back to a clean mathematical formula and a verifiable rule entry in RULES.md[cite: 1]. No black-box machine learning models or arbitrary heuristics[cite: 1].
  • Changing Rules Live: Because all logic is isolated inside pure functions in src/engine/rulesEngine.ts, you can alter FOIR percentages, repo rate baselines, or risk spreads in real time during the call and demonstrate immediate UI reactivity[cite: 1].
  • Honesty About Boundaries: If asked about balance sheet analysis, clearly articulate that the copilot relies on self-reported inputs and explicit uncertainty widening, avoiding false precision[cite: 1].
